Hermes AI Assistant

Hermes is a personal-use AI assistant configured by its owner to help manage tasks and workflows. Where authorised, it can interact with selected Google services through Google's OAuth authorisation process.

Google access is requested only when configured and authorised by the Google account holder. The services and permissions available depend on the application's configuration and the OAuth scopes granted.

This website provides information about the personal integration; it is not a public sign-up service or a hosted interface for the assistant.

For information about how Google data may be processed, please read the privacy policy.
