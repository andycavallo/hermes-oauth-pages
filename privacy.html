Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

About this application

Hermes AI Assistant is a privately configured, personal-use AI assistant. It is not offered as a public service. This policy describes its intended use with Google APIs; actual data handling also depends on the owner's active configuration and connected services.

Information accessed

When the account holder explicitly authorises access through Google OAuth, Hermes may access data from the Google services and scopes enabled for the integration. Depending on those permissions and the tasks requested, this may include Gmail messages and metadata, calendar information, or Google Drive files. The exact scopes should be reviewed in the Google consent screen.

Purpose and processing

Google user data is accessed to perform tasks requested or configured by the account holder, such as retrieving information, preparing summaries, or supporting personal workflows. Data may be processed by the assistant's hosting environment and, where configured, by external AI or integration providers. This page does not assert that Google data remains exclusively on the owner's device or server.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials, application logs, and task outputs may be stored in the environments used to operate Hermes. Their locations and retention periods depend on the actual deployment and should be reviewed by the account holder. This public page does not represent a verified inventory of those systems.

Sharing

Data is used to carry out the owner's configured workflows and may be transmitted to service providers required for those workflows. Hermes is not presented as a service for collecting or selling other users' Google data.

Control and revocation

The account holder can review or revoke Hermes's Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorised API access using the revoked grant but does not itself delete data already retained in other systems.

Contact and updates

This is a personal integration operated by its account holder. For questions or requests concerning the integration, contact the application operator through the support email shown on its Google OAuth consent screen. This policy should be updated if the integration's data handling changes.
